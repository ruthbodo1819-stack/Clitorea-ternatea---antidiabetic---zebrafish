# Evaluation of Antidiabetic Activity of *Clitoria ternatea* Extract Using a Glucose-Induced Zebrafish Embryo Model

## 📌 Project Overview
This mini-project evaluates the antidiabetic activity of *Clitoria ternatea* extract using a glucose-induced zebrafish embryo model.

The study includes phytochemical extraction, preliminary phytochemical screening, GC-MS analysis, toxicity assessment, and experimental evaluation of the extract 
using zebrafish embryos.

The project aims to explore the potential of *Clitoria ternatea* as a plant-based source of bioactive compounds with possible antidiabetic activity.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- To prepare an extract of *Clitoria ternatea*.
- To identify the major phytochemical constituents present in the extract.
- To analyze the extract using GC-MS.
- To assess the toxicity of the extract using zebrafish embryos.
- To evaluate the effect of the extract on a glucose-induced zebrafish embryo model.
- To investigate the potential antidiabetic activity of *Clitoria ternatea* extract.

---

## 🧪 Methodology

The major stages of the study include:

1. Collection and preparation of *Clitoria ternatea* plant material.
2. Extraction of phytochemicals from the plant material.
3. Preliminary phytochemical screening.
4. GC-MS analysis of the extract.
5. Preparation and maintenance of zebrafish embryos.
6. Induction of a glucose-associated hyperglycemic condition.
7. Toxicity assessment of the plant extract.
8. Treatment of the experimental groups with different concentrations of the extract.
9. Observation and evaluation of experimental responses.
10. Analysis and interpretation of the results.

---

## 🔬 Experimental Model

**Model organism:** Zebrafish (*Danio rerio*)

**Experimental model:** Glucose-induced zebrafish embryo model

Zebrafish embryos were used as an experimental model to investigate the biological effects of *Clitoria ternatea* extract under glucose-induced conditions.

---

## 🌿 Study Plant

**Plant:** *Clitoria ternatea*

*Clitoria ternatea* is a medicinal plant traditionally used in various systems of medicine and is known to contain several classes of phytochemicals, including flavonoids, phenolic compounds, and other bioactive constituents.

## 🧬 GC-MS Analysis

GC-MS analysis was performed to identify the chemical constituents present in the plant extract.

The analysis provided a profile of compounds detected in the extract and helped in the preliminary characterization of its chemical composition.

The identified compounds may provide a basis for further investigation of the biological activity of the extract.
